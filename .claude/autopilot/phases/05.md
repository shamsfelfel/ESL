## 1. Audit it before you build

- The passage: count the words. Heading, `passage_words` and your count must
  agree, and it must be inside 500–535.
- The five recalled words: check each one against **Lesson 10's committed
  `lesson.json`**. This is the check most likely to find something, because the
  brief's anchor list for Lesson 10 is not the fourteen that got written.
- The fourteen key words: all in the passage, none already taught in Lessons 1
  to 11 (`python scripts/course_state.py --words`).
- Part 04: does it open with the come-back to Lesson 10's past perfect,
  expanded into the continuous? Does it teach that the two commas change the
  meaning? Is there a *which* commenting on a whole clause?
- Are there at least two non-defining clauses in the passage itself, before
  part 04 names them?
- Part 06: ten questions, eight types, distractors wrong for nameable reasons.
- Part 10: do the two self-report questions ask something whose answer is
  usable — the length, and which grammar to revisit?

Fix what is wrong in `lesson.json`, re-run `--check`, report every change.

## 2. Build it

    cd lessons/lesson-12 && python build.py --build

## 3. Read what came out

# Lesson 10 — An Hour Too Late

**Level:** B1+ (tenth step)
**Topic:** A shop's accounts, a disk that failed, and a backup that finished too late
**Time:** 6 sessions, 50–60 minutes each — about 305 minutes of work in all
**Skills:** Listening → Reading → Vocabulary → Grammar → Comprehension → Speaking → Writing
**New in this lesson:** the past perfect, taught against the past simple to put two past actions in order, with by the time, already, just and never before; the third conditional, the same form doing a second job; and the weak had, the 'd and would have said as woulda. The passage stays at about 500 words for the fifth lesson running, and the question set is unchanged.

## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio more than any other part in this lesson, because a third conditional is nine words on paper and two in speech.
3. Read section 3.4 of part 04 twice. The past perfect is easy to make and easy to overuse, and 3.4 is where you learn when **not** to use it.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished. The five games come days later, not the same evening.

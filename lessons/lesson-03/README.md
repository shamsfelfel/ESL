## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio, because the weak sounds of *was* and *were* cannot be heard from the page.
3. Record your speaking answers on your phone, and send them with your writing and your self-report.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished.

## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio most of all, because *will* shrinks to a single consonant and *going to* becomes one word.
3. Part 06 is the same ten questions, in the same eight types, as Lesson 5. Nothing in the format is new — only the text. Do it in one sitting, with a clock.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished. The five games come days later, not the same evening.

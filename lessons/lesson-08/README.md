## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio most of all, because *would* and *had* arrive as the same single sound.
3. Read the **Come back to** section before the conditionals, not after. The rule about *when* and *until* is the same rule the *if*-clause follows, and the conditionals are far easier once it is in place.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished. The five games come days later, not the same evening.

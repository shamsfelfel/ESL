## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio most of all, because *can* and *can't* are told apart by a vowel, not by the *t*.
3. Section 3.5 of the grammar sheet is the one to read twice: *must not* and *do not have to* are opposites, and the passage uses both within four lines of each other.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished. The five games come days later, not the same evening.

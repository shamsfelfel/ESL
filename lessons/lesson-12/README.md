# Lesson 12 — The Room That Keeps the Light

**Level:** B1+ → B2 (twelfth step)
**Topic:** A computer room that opens on the hours there is power, and the people in it
**Time:** 6 sessions, 50–60 minutes each — about 305 minutes of work in all
**Skills:** Listening → Reading → Vocabulary → Grammar → Comprehension → Speaking → Writing
**New in this lesson:** relative clauses — who, which, that, whose and where, the object pronoun that can be left out, and the non-defining clause between two commas, which never takes that; which commenting on a whole clause, the first B2 structure in the course; the past perfect continuous against the simple; and the stop and fall you can hear at a comma, and had been said as one short sound. The passage stays at about 500 words for the seventh lesson running, and the question set is unchanged.

## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio more than any other part in this lesson, because it is the one place in the course where you can hear a comma.
3. Read section 3.5 of part 04 twice. Two commas change what a sentence claims, and 3.5 is where you write both versions and say what each one means.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished. Answer the two questions at the end of part 10 honestly: the next lessons are written from them. The five games come days later, not the same evening.

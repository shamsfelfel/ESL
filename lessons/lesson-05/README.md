# Lesson 5 — The Two O'Clock Class

**Level:** A2+ → B1 (fifth step)
**Topic:** Teaching computers, volunteering and small steady progress
**Time:** 6 sessions, 50–60 minutes each — about 305 minutes of work in all
**Skills:** Listening → Reading → Vocabulary → Grammar → Comprehension → Speaking → Writing
**New in this lesson:** the present perfect continuous with for and since, the -ing ending and the weak been, a longer passage of 481 words, and the last new question type: the writer's attitude. The comprehension set reaches ten questions, the size of a real TOEFL set.

## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order. Part 04 is grammar and has no audio; part 05 needs its audio most of all, because *has*, *been* and *-ing* all shrink in speech.
3. Part 06 has ten questions this time. Do it in one sitting, with a clock, the way the exam will be.
4. Open part 11, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 10 are finished.

## How to work through this lesson

1. Play **01-passage.mp3** and listen twice with no text in front of you. Do not open the PDF yet.
2. Then work through the parts in number order, one part at a time. Play the MP3 of a part before you open its sheet.
3. Record your speaking answers on your phone, and send them with your writing and your self-report.
4. Open part 09, the answer key, only when parts 01 to 08 are finished.
